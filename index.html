The app, in eight pictures

What it does

Made for pay that changes every day.

For servers and bartenders first, and for anyone paid by the job: drivers, stylists, inspectors, crews. Each job asks only for what it pays: hourly, tips, per job, delivery pay, commission or a day rate, or a mix.

New entry

Log a shift in seconds.

Cash tips, card tips, tip-out and hours: four numbers and you're done. Macros log your usual shift or job in one tap. Clock in and out, or just type the hours.

  • Your take-home and real hourly rate on every entry
  • Tip-out worked out from your job's rules, a percent of sales or of tips
  • A reminder to log it when a planned shift ends

The truth about your pay

Your real hourly rate, not a guess.

Every entry shows what you took home and what that made an hour. Your tips as a percent of sales. Overtime counted for you, by the week or by the day.

  • A weekly goal, and how far you are from it
  • Pay periods show what's paid and what's unpaid, with a summary to share
  • Search every entry by claim number, client or note

History

Every day's total, on the calendar.

Each day's total right on the month. Tap a day to see what you made and how. A dot says what else the day held: a trip, or something you spent.

  • Entries, pay periods and expenses, each on its own page
  • Each day's entries under the month, with their hours and miles

Schedule

Plan your week.

Plan shifts Mon–Fri, on weekends or on any days you pick, and see what the rest of the month should pay at your average. Appointments keep their claim number and client.

  • Your next shift on Home, and on your watch
  • A planned shift logged with one tap once it's over

Insights Pro extras

Know your best days.

Your weeks and months, what you made and what you kept, your average week and your real hourly rate. With Tipbit Pro: your best days, cash vs. card, by job, and your personal bests.

Trips Pro extras

Miles counted as you drive.

Miles at the IRS rate for the day you drove, or at your own rate. With Tipbit Pro, tap Start trip and Tipbit counts the miles until you tap Stop, with the route on a map. It never tracks miles on its own.

  • Type the miles on any entry, free
  • What they're worth beside them, never added to your pay unless your job pays you back for them

The year Pro extras

Ready for tax time.

The year at a glance: tips, wages and overtime, other pay, miles and spending. Expenses by category, with a photo of the receipt, and what you kept after what you spent.

  • With Tipbit Pro, statements and a year summary as PDFs, and a spreadsheet of your entries and expenses
  • Totals of what you logged, never tax advice

Everywhere you are

On your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Your entries stay in step through your own iCloud on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, with nothing to set up. Light and dark on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

On Apple Watch

Today's total, your week and your goal's ring at a glance. Log a shift with a tap, or type the tips on a number pad. Clock in and out on your wrist, one clock with your iPhone. Your next shift and your last five entries, and the week's total and your goal's ring on the watch face.

Private

Yours alone.

No account to make, no ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours. The privacy policy says exactly what goes where.

  • No sign-up and no Tipbit account
  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party code
  • Sync through your own private iCloud only

Free and Pro

Free to use. Pro when you want more.

Everything you log is free, with no limit on entries. Tipbit Pro adds more jobs, more macros and the extras. If you ever cancel, everything you made stays.
