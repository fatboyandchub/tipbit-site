What it does

Made for pay that changes every day.

For servers and bartenders first, and for anyone paid by the job: drivers, stylists, inspectors, crews.

And on Apple Watch: today's total and your goal's ring, tips logged with a tap or on a number pad, the clock, and your next shift.

Log it in seconds

Cash and card tips, tip-out and hours, or your usual shift in one tap. Each job asks only for what it pays.

The truth about your pay

Your take-home and real hourly rate on every entry, tip-out from your job's rules, overtime counted for you.

Every day on the calendar

Each day's total on the month, pay periods that show what's unpaid, and a schedule for the week ahead.

Miles and expenses

Miles at the IRS rate for the day you drove, expenses with a photo of the receipt, and The year for tax time.

Private

Yours alone.

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours. The privacy policy says exactly what goes where.

Free and Pro

Free to use. Pro when you want more.
