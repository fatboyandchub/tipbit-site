What it does

Made for pay that changes every day.

For servers and bartenders first, and anyone paid by the job: drivers, stylists, inspectors, crews. Each job asks only for what it pays:

  • Hourly
  • Tips
  • Per job
  • Delivery pay
  • Commission
  • Day rate
  • or a mix

The app, screen by screen

New entryLog a shift in seconds.

Cash tips, card tips, tip-out and hours: four numbers and you're done. Macros log your usual shift or job in one tap. Clock in and out, or type the hours. A reminder to log it when a planned shift ends.

Your real payYour real hourly rate, not a guess.

Every entry shows your take-home and what it made an hour. Tip-out from your job's rules, a percent of sales or of tips. Your tips as a percent of sales. Overtime counted for you, by the week or by the day.

Goal and pay periodsPaid, unpaid, and how far to your goal.

A weekly goal, and how far you are from it. Pay periods show what's paid and what's unpaid, with a summary to share. Search every entry by claim number, client or note.

HistoryEvery day's total, on the calendar.

Each day's total right on the month. Tap a day to see what you made and how, each entry with its hours and miles. A dot marks a trip, or something you spent. Entries, pay periods and expenses, each on its own page.

SchedulePlan your week.

Shifts Mon–Fri, on weekends or on any days you pick, and what the rest of the month should pay at your average. Appointments keep their claim number and client. Your next shift on Home and on your watch; one tap logs it once it's over.

Insights Pro extrasKnow your best days.

Your weeks and months, what you made and what you kept, your average week and your real hourly rate. With Tipbit Pro: best days, cash vs. card, by job, and personal bests.

Trips Pro extrasMiles counted as you drive.

Type the miles on any entry, free, at the IRS rate for the day you drove or at your own rate; their worth sits beside them, never added to your pay unless your job pays you back for them. With Tipbit Pro, Start trip counts the miles until you tap Stop, with the route on a map. It never tracks miles on its own.

The year Pro extrasReady for tax time.

Tips, wages and overtime, other pay, miles and spending, at a glance. Expenses by category, with a photo of the receipt, and what you kept after what you spent. With Tipbit Pro, statements and a year summary as PDFs, and a spreadsheet of your entries and expenses. Totals of what you logged, never tax advice.

Everywhere you are

On your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Your entries stay in step through your own iCloud on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, with nothing to set up. Light and dark on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

On Apple Watch

Today's total, your week and your goal's ring at a glance; log a shift with a tap, or type the tips on a number pad. Clock in and out on your wrist, one clock with your iPhone. Your next shift, your last five entries, and the week's total and your goal's ring on the watch face.

Private

Yours alone.

No account to make, no ads, no tracking. Your entries stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours. The privacy policy says exactly what goes where.

  • No sign-up and no Tipbit account
  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party code
  • Sync through your own private iCloud only
